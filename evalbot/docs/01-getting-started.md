# Getting started

Lumen Notes is a note-taking app for desktop and mobile. Create an account with your email address, then choose New notebook to start writing.

The Free plan includes up to 5 notebooks and 100 MB of storage. The Pro plan costs $4 per month and includes unlimited notebooks and 10 GB of storage.

Lumen Notes runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android. There is no Windows Phone app.
